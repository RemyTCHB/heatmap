# Viewer Guide

This guide is for everyone at TCHB: what the Deals Heat Map shows and how to read it. Remy, Monz and Jan can also change deals; their Editor Guide adds those parts.

## Getting Started

Open **heatmap.totalchb.com** and click **Sign In With Google**. Use your company Google account (totalchb.com or kpidriver.com). The page opens on the whole picture: totals at the top, the map, the deal list, the charts and the leaderboards.

![The top of the page: the live clock, the totals and the filters](overview.jpg)

The header works like the Hub's:

- **The clock** is the live status. Green means everything is saved and up to date. Blue means a change is being saved. Red means something did not save: click the clock and press **Retry Now**.
- **Active** shows how many people have the page open. Click it to see who, and whether they are on a phone or a computer.
- **The sun and moon** switch between the light and dark look.
- **Your photo** opens your menu: Settings, Team Members, Help and Sign Out.

![Click the clock for the last sync time](live-clock.jpg)

![Click Active to see who is online](active.jpg)

The page checks for new deals every minute, so a deal that signs, closes or cancels in GoHighLevel shows up on its own. You never need to reload.

## The Totals

The tiles under the title count the deals that match the filters:

- **Total Deals**, **Signed · Open**, **Closed** and **Cancelled**.
- **Close Rate**: closed deals out of every deal that has finished (closed or cancelled). Open deals do not count until they finish.
- **Actual Profit (Revenue)**: the profit from every closing, Fix & Flip resales included.
- **Avg Profit Per Close**: actual profit divided by the closed deals.

## Filtering

Everything on the page follows the filters: the totals, the map, the deal list, the charts, the leaderboards and Where To Market. The filters stay pinned under the header as you scroll.

- **All, Signed, Closed, Cancelled**: click one to see only those deals; click more to combine them; **All** brings back everything.
- **Needs Review**: only the deals that have something to check (see A Deal's Details).
- **The period**: All Time, This Year, Last 90 Days, Last 30 Days, or **Custom Range**. A deal counts in the period of its last event (the day it closed or cancelled, or the day it signed if it is still open).
- **ACQ, DISPO, FUS, Lead Source and Exit**: pick one to see only their deals.
- **A state**: click a state on the map to see only its deals. The state shows as a chip in the filters; click its × to clear it.

### Picking A Custom Range

Choose **Custom Range…** in the period filter. Click the first day, then the last day; the days between are shaded. Use the arrows to move between months, then press **Apply**. Choose **All Time** to clear it. To change the range, open Custom Range again.

![The custom range calendar, like the Hub's due-date picker](custom-range.jpg)

## The Map

The map has three views. Switch between them at its top left.

### Pins

Every deal is a pin at its street address. The shape and colour tell you where it stands:

| Pin | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Blue circle | Signed, still open |
| Green diamond | Closed |
| Red X | Cancelled |
| A blue circle with a blue ring pulsing around it | Signed in the last 14 days |

Hover a pin for the deal's details; click it to open the deal. State initials sit on every state, and the small northeastern states appear as you zoom in.

![Pins: every deal at its address, with state initials](map-pins.jpg)

### Heat

The map is split into hexagons, each shaded by the deals inside it: the darker, the more. Use the menu next to the views to shade by **Deals**, **Actual Profit** or **Close Rate**. Hover a hexagon for its numbers and its top cities; click it to zoom in on that area.

![Heat: hexagons shaded by deals](map-heat.jpg)

### States

Each state is shaded by the measure you pick: deals, actual profit, close rate or cancel rate. Hover a state and it grows out with its numbers; click it to filter the page to that state.

![States: each state shaded by the measure you pick](map-states.jpg)

### Zooming And Moving

- On a computer: hold **Ctrl** (or **⌘** on a Mac) and scroll, or pinch on a trackpad. A plain scroll moves the page, not the map. Drag to move a zoomed map.
- On a phone: use **two fingers** to zoom and move the map. One finger scrolls the page.
- The **+**, **−** and **⤢** buttons zoom in, zoom out and show the whole country again.

### Latest Activity

Beside the map, the newest signed, closed and cancelled deals are listed. Click one to open it.

## The Deal List

Under the map, every deal that matches the filters is listed: Last Event first, then Signed, Status, Address, City, State, Lead Source, ACQ, DISPO, FUS, Potential, Actual and Exit Strategy. Click a heading to sort by it (click again to flip the order). Click a row to open the deal. A red ⚑ line under an address means the deal needs review.

![The deal list](deal-list.jpg)

On a phone, the list shows as cards; press **Show More** at the bottom for more deals.

## A Deal's Details

Clicking a pin, a row or a Latest Activity item opens the deal on the right: its status, address, lead source, people, profits and dates.

![A deal, as everyone sees it](deal-panel-view.jpg)

- **Fix & Flips** show both closings: the purchase and the resale, each with its date and profit. Actual profit is the two added together.
- **Feedback** is the general Feedback field from GoHighLevel. **Cancel Reason** is the feedback a deal carried when it was cancelled.
- **History** lists what happened and who did it: GoHighLevel for the hooks, CSV History for the deals loaded from the old sheets, or the name of the person who changed it.
- **Needs Review** at the top lists anything to check, such as a closed deal with no DISPO.

## How Deals Arrive

Deals come from GoHighLevel. When a deal moves to **Contract Signed**, **Closed** or **Cancelled**, GoHighLevel sends it here, and the deal is matched by its **contact ID**.

- **The date of each event is the day it happens.** A deal signed today shows signed today; a deal that closes tomorrow shows closed tomorrow.
- **Closed** records the profit from **AB Profit (TC)**.
- **A second Closed** on the same deal is a **Fix & Flip** resale: its profit (**BC Profit**) is added to the deal.
- **Cancelled** keeps the **Feedback** as the cancel reason.
- A new lead source or exit strategy from GoHighLevel is added to the lists on its own, marked **New From CRM**.
- Deals from before go-live came from the old Signed, Cancelled and Revenues sheets and are marked **CSV History**.

## Month By Month

Two charts show the last 12 months: **Deals Per Month** (signed, closed and cancelled side by side) and **Profit Per Month** (with the 12-month total). Hover a month for its numbers. They follow the filters.

![Month By Month](month-by-month.jpg)

## Leaderboards

Fifteen boards rank the team and the markets: Top ACQ, Top DISPO, Top FUS, Top Cities, Top States, Highest Avg Profit Per State, Top Lead Sources, Where We Lose Deals, Cancels By ACQ, Speed To Contract, Exit Strategy Mix, Best ACQ + DISPO Pairs, Biggest Deals, Fastest To Close and Open Deals By State.

- The small switch on a board changes what it ranks by (for example Profit, Closes or Close %).
- **Show Top 10** lists more.
- Rates only count people and places with enough finished deals to be fair (the board says how many).
- Click a state on Top States to filter the page to it; click a deal on Biggest Deals to open it.

![Leaderboards](leaderboards.jpg)

## Where To Market

Three cards turn the numbers into suggestions. They compare each area with the team as a whole and follow the filters.

- **Market More Here**: states and cities that close more often than the team and make more per close, with the lead source that brought the most profit there.
- **Pull Back Here**: areas with a low close rate, or that close but make well under the team's average.
- **Fix The Fall-Outs**: cancel reasons grouped into themes (the seller backed out, price, title or probate, repairs, no buyer, financing, an agent or another offer, could not reach the seller), each with a way to prevent it, and examples. Until cancel reasons arrive, it shows where deals fall out by lead source, ACQ and state.

![Where To Market](where-to-market.jpg)

## Team Members

Your photo → **Team Members** shows the team, mirrored from the Hub's Team Members: Managers, then each department, with photos, roles and emails. **Can Edit** marks the people who can change deals here. People who are on deals but not in the Hub show at the bottom: **No Hub Login** (such as the AI agents) and **Former Members**.

![Team Members, mirrored from the Hub](team-members.jpg)

The team refreshes once a day. Press **Refresh From Hub** to pull it now. Add, move or rename people in the Hub, not here.

## On A Phone

The page works on a phone the same way:

- The header shows the round TCHB mark, the clock and your photo. **Who's Online**, the light and dark switch, Settings, Team Members, Help and Sign Out are in your photo menu.
- The filters scroll sideways. The deal list shows as cards.
- On the map, two fingers zoom and move it.

## Troubleshooting

- **The clock is red.** Something did not save or the page lost its connection. Click the clock and press **Retry Now**.
- **"This account is not allowed."** Sign in with your company Google account (totalchb.com or kpidriver.com).
- **The Google window closes straight away.** Your browser blocked the pop-up. Allow pop-ups for heatmap.totalchb.com and try again.
- **A deal is missing from the map.** It has no street address the map can find. It is still in the deal list with a Needs Review note; Remy, Monz or Jan can fix its address.
- **Something looks out of date.** The page refreshes every minute; reload it if you want it now.
