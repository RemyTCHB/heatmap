# Editor Guide

This guide is for Remy, Monz and Jan, the people who can change contracts. It starts with the editing tools, then covers everything in the Viewer Guide.

## Who Can Edit

Only **Remy, Monz and Jan** can fix, add or delete contracts, clear review flags, and change the Settings lists. Everyone else at TCHB sees the same page read-only. Your photo menu shows **Can Edit** under your name. Every change you make is saved for the whole team at once, and the contract's history records your name.

![The photo menu: Settings, Team Members, Help and Sign Out](photo-menu.jpg)

## Fixing A Contract

Click a pin, a row in the contract list or a Latest Activity item to open the contract. Change what is wrong and press **Save Changes**. Changed fields are highlighted until you save, and the button counts your changes.

![A contract open for editing](deal-panel.jpg)

- **Status**: Signed, Closed or Cancelled. Fix it here if GoHighLevel sent the wrong one. Cancelled sets actual revenue to $0; Signed clears the closing date.
- **Address**: typing a 5-digit **ZIP** fills in the city and state. When you change the address, the pin moves to the new street address after you save (or to the ZIP when the address cannot be found). The contract list shows the address on one line, but the street, city, state and ZIP stay separate fields here, the same as they arrive from GoHighLevel.
- **Lead Source, Exit Strategy, ACQ, DISPO, FUS**: pick from the lists. **Edit List** (or **From The Hub**) opens that list in Settings.
- **Revenue and dates**: type the numbers; negative revenue is allowed for a loss.
- **Feedback and Cancel Reason**: notes. The cancel reason feeds Fix The Fall-Outs in Where To Market.

Edits change the Heat Map only; they do not write back to GoHighLevel.

## Adding A Contract

Press **+ Add Contract** above the contract list. Fill in what you know: the street address is the only must. Type the ZIP to fill in the city and state, pick the status and the people, and press **Add Contract**. The pin goes on the map at the street address. The contract is marked **Added** in the list, and its history says you added it.

![Adding a contract](add-deal.jpg)

## Deleting A Contract

Open the contract, press **Delete**, then **Delete Contract** to confirm. It is removed for everyone and cannot be brought back, so use it for duplicates and mistakes only.

## Needs Review

Some contracts carry a ⚑ note to check: a closed contract with no DISPO (set to **TCHB** for now), a contract with no signed record, two addresses that matched loosely, a cancel that arrived after a close, an address the map cannot find, and so on. **Needs Review** in the filters shows only those contracts.

- Open the contract: its notes are at the top.
- Fix the contract and save; most notes clear themselves (picking the real DISPO clears the TCHB note, adding a ZIP clears the map note).
- Press **Mark Fixed** next to a note once you have checked it and nothing needs changing.

## Settings: The Lists

Your photo → **Settings** holds the dropdown lists. Each tab shows how many contracts use each option.

### Lead Sources And Exit Strategies

![Settings: Lead Sources](settings-lists.jpg)

- **Rename**: click a name, type the new one and press Enter. Every contract and report follows, and the old spelling keeps matching GoHighLevel.
- **Add**: type a name at the bottom and press **Add**.
- **Remove**: press the bin. An option no contract uses goes at once; for one that contracts use, pick another option to move them to, then **Move & Remove**. Its spellings then match the option you moved them to.
- **New From CRM**: GoHighLevel sent a value that was not on the list, so it was added for you. Keep it (press the badge to clear it), rename it, or remove it and move its contracts to the right option.

### ACQ, DISPO And FUS

![Settings: the FUS people](settings-people.jpg)

People come from the Hub's Team Members. Each tab has up to four parts:

- **From The Hub**: the department's people in the Hub. Change them in the Hub.
- **Not In The Hub**: names on contracts that match no one in the Hub. **Link** one to the right person (its contracts move to them and the spelling keeps matching), or keep it as **No Hub Login** or **Former Member**.
- **No Hub Login**: real people or agents without a Hub account, such as the AI SMS and AI CALL agents.
- **Former Members**: people who left; their names stay on their contracts.

**Undo** moves someone back to Not In The Hub.
