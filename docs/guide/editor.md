# Editor Guide

This guide is for Remy, Monz and Jan, the people who can change deals. It starts with the editing tools, then covers everything in the Viewer Guide.

## Who Can Edit

Only **Remy, Monz and Jan** can fix, add or delete deals, clear review flags, and change the Settings lists. Everyone else at TCHB sees the same page read-only. Your photo menu shows **Can Edit** under your name. Every change you make is saved for the whole team at once, and the deal's history records your name.

![The photo menu: Settings, Team Members, Help and Sign Out](photo-menu.jpg)

## Fixing A Deal

Click a pin, a row in the deal list or a Latest Activity item to open the deal. Change what is wrong and press **Save Changes**. Changed fields are highlighted until you save, and the button counts your changes.

![A deal open for editing](deal-panel.jpg)

- **Status**: Signed, Closed or Cancelled. Fix it here if GoHighLevel sent the wrong one. Cancelled sets actual profit to $0; Signed clears the closing date.
- **Address**: typing a 5-digit **ZIP** fills in the city and state. When you change the address, the pin moves to the new street address after you save (or to the ZIP when the address cannot be found).
- **Lead Source, Exit Strategy, ACQ, DISPO, FUS**: pick from the lists. **Edit List** (or **From The Hub**) opens that list in Settings.
- **Profits and dates**: type the numbers; negative profit is allowed for a loss.
- **Feedback and Cancel Reason**: notes. The cancel reason feeds Fix The Fall-Outs in Where To Market.

Edits change the Heat Map only; they do not write back to GoHighLevel.

## Adding A Deal

Press **+ Add Deal** (in the filters, or above the deal list). Fill in what you know: the street address is the only must. Type the ZIP to fill in the city and state, pick the status and the people, and press **Add Deal**. The pin goes on the map at the street address. The deal is marked **Added** in the list, and its history says you added it.

![Adding a deal](add-deal.jpg)

## Deleting A Deal

Open the deal, press **Delete**, then **Delete Deal** to confirm. It is removed for everyone and cannot be brought back, so use it for duplicates and mistakes only.

## Needs Review

Some deals carry a ⚑ note to check: a closed deal with no DISPO (set to **TCHB** for now), a deal with no signed record, two addresses that matched loosely, a cancel that arrived after a close, an address the map cannot find, and so on. **Needs Review** in the filters shows only those deals.

- Open the deal: its notes are at the top.
- Fix the deal and save; most notes clear themselves (picking the real DISPO clears the TCHB note, adding a ZIP clears the map note).
- Press **Mark Fixed** next to a note once you have checked it and nothing needs changing.

## Settings: The Lists

Your photo → **Settings** holds the dropdown lists. Each tab shows how many deals use each option.

### Lead Sources And Exit Strategies

![Settings: Lead Sources](settings-lists.jpg)

- **Rename**: click a name, type the new one and press Enter. Every deal and report follows, and the old spelling keeps matching GoHighLevel.
- **Add**: type a name at the bottom and press **Add**.
- **Remove**: press the bin. An option no deal uses goes at once; for one that deals use, pick another option to move them to, then **Move & Remove**. Its spellings then match the option you moved them to.
- **New From CRM**: GoHighLevel sent a value that was not on the list, so it was added for you. Keep it (press the badge to clear it), rename it, or remove it and move its deals to the right option.

### ACQ, DISPO And FUS

![Settings: the FUS people](settings-people.jpg)

People come from the Hub's Team Members. Each tab has up to four parts:

- **From The Hub**: the department's people in the Hub. Change them in the Hub.
- **Not In The Hub**: names on deals that match no one in the Hub. **Link** one to the right person (its deals move to them and the spelling keeps matching), or keep it as **No Hub Login** or **Former Member**.
- **No Hub Login**: real people or agents without a Hub account, such as the AI SMS and AI CALL agents.
- **Former Members**: people who left; their names stay on their deals.

**Undo** moves someone back to Not In The Hub.
